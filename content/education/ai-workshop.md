tracks:
  - key: ks3
    eyebrow: KS3 Track · Years 7–9
    title: Meet AI, make things, understand the world
    intro: >-
      Six guided, hands-on sessions that build real intuition about AI — playful,
      age-appropriate, and grounded in what students actually use.
    sessions:
      - n: 1
        title: Meet AI
        desc: Interview a chatbot, try to trick it, spot AI in daily life — games, music, social feeds.
      - n: 2
        title: Talking to AI properly
        desc: "The 'prompt detective' game: compare badly vs well-worded requests and see the difference."
      - n: 3
        title: AI as a homework helper
        desc: Use AI to explain tricky topics, then fact-check it against a textbook. It can be confidently wrong.
      - n: 4
        title: Making something with AI
        desc: Generate artwork, illustrate a comic strip, or build a simple chatbot personality. Students' favourite session.
      - n: 5
        title: Is AI fair? Is it safe?
        featured: true
        desc: Spot bias in image generation. Discuss data privacy, what not to share, and why copying AI answers isn't learning.
      - n: 6
        title: Show and tell
        desc: Students present their session 4 project to the group and parents, explaining what the AI did and what they did.
  - key: ks45
    eyebrow: KS4/5 Track · Years 10–13
    title: Applied AI skills for study, work and beyond
    intro: >-
      Six sessions that treat AI as a practical tool — for coursework, research, creative
      projects and career readiness, with critical thinking built in throughout.
    sessions:
      - n: 1
        title: What AI is, and isn't
        desc: Compare a rule-based chatbot vs. a generative model on the same prompts. Build real intuition fast.
      - n: 2
        title: Prompting as a skill
        desc: Iterate on a real task — summarise a topic, draft a personal statement — and see how specificity changes output.
      - n: 3
        title: AI for research & study
        desc: Generate revision notes and practice questions from real coursework, then verify AI output for accuracy.
      - n: 4
        title: Creative & applied project
        desc: "Choose a mini-project: a no-code app, an AI-generated presentation, a portfolio piece. Build it."
      - n: 5
        title: Ethics, bias & limitations
        featured: true
        desc: Test for bias, discuss academic integrity, data privacy, and what AI means for the careers ahead of them.
      - n: 6
        title: Capstone showcase
        desc: Present the session 4 project to the group and parents — explaining what AI did and what they did themselves.
